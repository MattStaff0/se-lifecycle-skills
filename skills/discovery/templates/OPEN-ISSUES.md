# Open Issues

**Project:** _[name]_

_[What this file is for: every question about the project nobody can answer yet, with the person who can. A spec with confident guesses in its gaps is more dangerous than one with holes, because nobody can tell the guesses from the facts. Writing "we don't know" is the correct state early on; knowing and not writing it down is the failure._

_**Where entries come from:** drafting a document and hitting something the sources don't say; the agent's list of questions it couldn't answer; a conversation where someone says "I'd have to check"._

_**How they leave:** answered in a conversation, a message, or a document. Move the row to Resolved and put the substance in the document that owns it (an objective, a glossary term, a rule, a contract). This file is a queue, not a home._

_**Identifiers** are numbers, `OI-1` upward, never renumbered: this list only grows at the bottom.]_

## Before the next conversation

_[Pick the shortlist by what it costs to stay wrong, not by what is easy to ask: a question whose wrong guess sends the team the wrong way for a month comes first. Skip anything you can settle by reading a document or trying the current tool yourself. Send the shortlist the day before.]_
