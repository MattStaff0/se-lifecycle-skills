## 4. How work is split

Work is split **by slice, not by layer**: one person owns a slice end to end (design, code, tests, data checks, close-out). Nobody is "the front-end person" or "the data person" for someone else's slice.

_[How a slice is claimed (assign the issue, move the card).]_

## 6. Working with AI

Every member can explain any line submitted under their name. Agent output nobody has read is not merged. The charter for the agent lives in `AGENTS.md`.

_[Which tools the team uses, and any limits: data that never goes into a prompt, models or features that are off limits.]_

## 7. When a commitment slips

When someone misses something, any member raises it, in four steps, in this order:

1. **Observe:** say only what can be checked (commits, the board, the meeting notes), not impressions.
2. **Analyze:** say what it affects: what is blocked, which date is at risk.
3. **Investigate:** ask before assuming a cause.
4. **Fix:** agree on one action with an owner and a date, and write it down.

_[Who raises it and how soon. What the team does if it happens again. Who you escalate to after that (a manager, a TA, an instructor), and that you escalate early.]_
